We fix homes for neighbors who need a hand.

Generations Construction is a nonprofit that repairs homes for under-resourced families across Northern Colorado. We pay for that work by giving leftover building materials a second life.

Request a repair Donate materials

Leftover materials become repaired homes

Construction sites throw away good lumber, doors and fixtures every day. We put them to work.

  1. Materials come in

    Contractors, builders and homeowners donate surplus lumber, fixtures and supplies instead of hauling them to the landfill.

  2. We sort and sell

    Usable materials go to our reclaimed materials yard, priced well below new, for anyone working on a project.

  3. Sales fund repairs

    All proceeds pay for repairs on the homes of families who couldn't otherwise afford them.

Some of those same materials end up in the homes we repair.

Home repair

We focus on repairs that keep a home safe, dry and livable, so the people in it can stay.

  • Roof and gutter repairs
  • Steps, railings and ramps
  • Floors and subfloors
  • Doors and windows
  • Decks and porches
  • Grab bars and safety fixes

Who we help

Homeowners anywhere in Northern Colorado who are facing a repair they can't manage on their own: seniors, people living with a disability, and low-income families. Not sure if you qualify? Ask us anyway.

Reclaimed materials

Every board that comes through our yard either goes into a repair or is sold to fund one. Either way, it stays out of the landfill.

Donate materials

Leftovers from a job, a remodel or a garage cleanout are welcome. Contractors with job-site surplus can ask about pickup. We'll give you a receipt for your records.

We take

  • Lumber and plywood
  • Decking
  • Doors
  • Windows
  • Cabinets
  • Tile and flooring
  • Plumbing and light fixtures
  • Hardware and fasteners
  • Tools

We can't take

  • Paint and chemicals
  • Broken glass
  • Rotted or moldy wood
  • Anything with asbestos
